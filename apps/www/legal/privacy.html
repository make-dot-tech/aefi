Privacy

Last updated 2026-09-15.

aefi indexes publicly observable Arc contract events (transfers, memos, identity and job registries) to build an evidence graph. Wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and onchain metadata in that graph are public blockchain data, not secrets we collect from you.

This website and Evidence Studio may log standard request metadata (IP, user agent, path, timing) via our hosting and CDN providers to operate, debug, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information. We do not require an account to use the public studio or docs.

If you email augustine@aefi.io, we keep that correspondence as needed to reply.

API callers are responsible for what they send in request bodies. Do not submit private keys or offchain secrets to aefi.
